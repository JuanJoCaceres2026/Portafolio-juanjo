Profile & Background

About Me

I’m a General High School student and a participant in ¡SUPÉRATE! ADOC, where I study technology, English, and personal development. I’m interested in technology that helps people: noticing a need, imagining a solution, and shaping it into a clear digital experience.

That curiosity has led me to projects such as PrimerPaso, a job-search prototype co-created with a classmate for adults looking for entry-level work, and TechGuide, a camera-based tool that recognizes electronic devices. These projects let me combine creativity, collaboration, and hands-on learning.

I’m curious, responsible, detail-oriented, and eager to keep learning. I have also taken part in community volunteer activities. I hope to keep growing in technology and contribute ideas that become useful tools for others.
